SPLATOON AUDIO CONVERTER
Extract WAVs, replace audio tracks or preview sounds.

GET STARTED
Extract the entire ZIP, then open Splatoon Audio Converter.exe.
Keep the _internal folder next to the EXE. Do not run it from inside the ZIP.

1. Open an audio file.
2. Select a track to listen to.
3. Choose a replacement .wav file.
4. Save as a new file. Your original files are not affected.

FILES AND PLAYBACK
Supports .bars, .bars.zs, .bwav, .bwav.zs, .bfstm and .bfstm.zs.
Click Play to listen, the circular arrow to replay, or the waveform to seek.
Preview volume supports 0-300%; louder settings may clip.
Multichannel music previews its first stereo pair. Extraction keeps all channels.
Replacement WAVs must be mono/stereo, uncompressed 16-bit PCM.
Replacing streams with more than two channels is not supported.
Older BARS containing FWAV/FSTP sounds are preview/extract only.

REQUIREMENTS
Windows 10/11, 64-bit, and an audio output device for listening.
Python, FFmpeg apps, Switch Toolbox and other converters are not needed.
Nintendo audio helpers need Microsoft .NET Framework 4.8, normally included
with current Windows installations. If missing, install it from Microsoft:
https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet-framework/net48

PRIVACY AND LICENSES
Works offline. No accounts, updater, telemetry or audio uploads.
No personal settings, paths, logs, fonts or game audio are included.
Only your theme choice is saved locally; previews are temporary.
